Spatial Operating System v0.3 — doctrine For planning authorities

Not an interface aesthetic —
a philosophy of urban intelligence.

v0.3 reframes the system again. v0.2 made it civic; v0.3 makes it spatial. Containers become districts and corridors. Density becomes cognitive zoning. Time enters the design language. Trust becomes visible. The interface stops referencing nature aesthetically — it starts thinking like a city.

Climate04

Monsoon logic — Singaporean spatial cognition

Generic biophilia could come from anywhere. v0.3 imports specifically Singaporean spatial habits: sheltered linkways (content under a covering rule), thresholds (the considered gap between zones), and emergence (content resolving from fog rather than fading in). These replace fades and panel-slides as default transitions for consequential content.

DoctrineClimatic intelligence, not poetry
Logic · 01

Sheltered linkway

Content sits beneath a clearly named cover. The cover is structural, not decorative — it carries a session ID, time, or governance scope.

S-04 · canopy The content here is protected by an explicit shelter — a bronze covering rule with crosshatching. Used for AI-assisted sections.
Logic · 02

Threshold

The considered gap between zones. Crossing it means a transition in tone, contrast, or governance — never silently.

Draftanalytical zone
Issuedcritical zone
Logic · 03

Emergence

Content resolves through fog, not through opacity. AI responses, scenario outputs, and live data appear by becoming legible — not by appearing whole.

Motion08

Four climatological curves

v0.3 adds a fourth — Reveal — for consequential content (AI responses, scenario outputs, approvals). It mimics monsoon clearing: a horizontal wipe with a slight pause at full reveal. No fade, no slide, no bounce.

Default duration200ms · slower on idle
Curve · 01

Settle

cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) · 200–420ms · state changes, layout shifts.

Curve · 02

Fog-pass

Linear · 5s+ · loading, idle. No spinners.

Curve · 03

Light-shift

ease-in-out · 4s · live state hint.

Curve · 04 · NEW

Reveal

monsoon-ease · 1.2–2s · consequential content emerging from fog. Horizontal wipe.

Trust10

Visible epistemic structure

AI in planning fails without traceability. v0.3 codifies a trust layer: every consequential AI surface carries source lineage, a confidence band, a policy badge, a temporal validity marker, and a review status strip. None of this is decoration. All of it is auditable.

Rule · 01
Citation is structural

Any consequential answer carries at least one source chip. Citation-less answers must be marked with a think indicator and excluded from approval flows.

Rule · 02
Confidence is visible, not whispered

A 5px gradient band, marker, and number. Below 0.65 → degraded state with warning tone. Below 0.45 → blocked from issued surfaces.

Rule · 03
Policy authority is named

Every AI claim that touches policy carries the governing document and version. "URA · 2024-12" is more useful than a soft confidence pill.

Rule · 04
Time-bound by default

Every answer has an explicit validity window. The system auto-flags claims past their re-run trigger and downranks them in retrieval.

Rule · 05
Review state is public

The four-stage strip (Drafted → Sourced → Reviewed → Cleared) is visible on every AI surface intended for governance. State changes are logged.

Maps11

Tropical civic cartography — refined

The map carries identity. Roads are drafting-style hairlines, transit lines are the only glow in the system, ecology is translucent strata, heat is bronze wash, AI overlays are flow particles, and labels are cartouches with embedded trust chips. The legend is a dark rail that matches the platform shell.

DoctrineMap-native AI, not AI on maps
Layer · 01

Roads — drafted

Hairline strokes in graphite-400. Major arteries gain weight (2px), never color. Drafting-board discipline.

Layer · 02

Transit — luminous veins

Cyan with 2–4px outer glow. The only licensed glow in the system. Stations appear as bordered dots.

Layer · 03

Ecology — translucent strata

Canopy as 40–55% green washes with hard 1px boundaries. Layered, not painted over.

Layer · 04

Heat — atmospheric depth

Surface temperature as bronze washes. Elevation as warm-to-cool grey gradient.

Layer · 05

AI overlay — flow

Predictions render as drifting particle vectors — pedestrian flow, monsoon shelter, simulated metabolism. They behave like weather, not like buttons.

Layer · 06

Cartouches — anchored

Cream rectangles with corner ticks, embedded trust chips, mono fields. Hand-placed, not floated.
